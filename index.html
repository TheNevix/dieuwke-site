Meaningful colours, limitless connections.

I believe colours can do more than just make things look pretty. They can tell stories, spark emotions and bring people together.

Inclusion is at the heart of everything I create. I want to create designs that are easy to read, understand and connect with, so as many people as possible can feel included.

My goal? To use colours and creativity to build bridges between people and break down barriers.

Because to me, great design is for everyone!

Dressed to Impress: A Penguin's Story!

How do you make a complex scientific topic fun and understandable for children? By dressing up penguins, of course! Through playful illustrations, thoughtful colour choices and visual storytelling, I transformed scientific information about clothing into an engaging and accessible A3 infographic.

Limo: A little sip of adventure!

How do you turn a healthy children's lemonade into a playful forest adventure? By creating a colourful brand filled with friendly characters, fruity flavours and nature-inspired details! From the logo and illustrations to the cans, six-pack packaging and campaign, I brought Limo to life through a recognisable and consistent visual identity.
